# Privacy Policy

Streak Keeper collects and stores account and streak data to provide app features.

## Data We Collect
- Account information from Firebase Authentication (email and user id)
- Streak and check-in data entered by users

## How We Use Data
- Authenticate users
- Store and display streak progress
- Calculate badges and statistics

## Data Storage
- Data is stored in Firebase Firestore under your authenticated account.

## Data Sharing
- We do not sell personal data.
- We use Firebase services to process and store app data.

## Data Deletion
- Users can delete streak history inside the app.
- For full account deletion requests, contact support.
